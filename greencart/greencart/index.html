Your Privacy Matters

GreenCart saves small files called cookies in your browser. Some are needed for the site to work. The others are optional, so you choose whether we can use them.

Essential Operations Keeps you signed in, remembers your cart and saves this choice. The site can't work without these. Always on
Performance & Analytics Counts page visits and loading times so we can make the site faster. Never your name or address.
Marketing & Offers Uses what you buy to show you seasonal offers and deals.

Accept All turns both optional types on. Reject All keeps only the essential ones. Save My Choices keeps the switches as you set them. You can change this anytime.
